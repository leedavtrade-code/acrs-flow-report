Critic: what the ledger says

  1. Setup A carries the system: 57 of 330 trades (17%) produce +167.2R of +178.8R. Without its 3 best trades it still makes +67.6R and it was positive in 5 of 6 years (bootstrap P(not better than the rest) = 0.000).
  2. Setup B is 64 trades (19% of all entries) for +7.1R in total (-5.6R without its best 3). It occupies slots and risk budget for roughly zero edge; this is a hypothesis to test in the builder at portfolio level, not a rule change.
  3. Setup C is 209 trades (63% of all entries) for +4.6R in total (-21.3R without its best 3). It occupies slots and risk budget for roughly zero edge; this is a hypothesis to test in the builder at portfolio level, not a rule change.
  4. Disaster-stop and gap exits cost -30.5R. Those entries were stretched: 12.3% above MA50 vs 4.7% for normal exits, RVOL 2.12 vs 1.36.
  5. Returns are tail-driven: the top 5% of trades supply 68% of gross profit. Any filter that skips entries has to be judged on R kept, not on hit rate.
  6. Live window: 29 closed trades, -5.41R, PF 0.36. Drawing 29 backtest trades that could have closed inside a 69-day window gives a result this bad 36% of the time (PF this low: 21%): normal for a young book. Comparing against all backtest trades, long winners included, would say 5% and wrongly suggest something broke. Only 2 Setup A entries vs 5.0 expected (P = 0.10); the live book has mostly been fed the setups that earn ~0R in the backtest too.
  7. Entry-feature drift vs backtest: none significant after Bonferroni (p < 0.0045). The machine is buying the same kind of stocks.
  8. ML entry filter (keep top 50% by model score), purged annual walk-forward: take-all +171.1R vs ridge +158.7R (IC -0.113) and logistic +125.8R (IC -0.059). Status: rejected - stays record-only.
  9. Deflated Sharpe: the 5-year curve's Sharpe 1.42 gives DSR 0.91 at the registry's 19 trials; it drops below 0.95 at about 10 independent trials. This is before the survivorship look-ahead the simulation page already measures, which lowers it further.

Step 3 · Deflated Sharpe: how many tries can this backtest afford?

Sharpe 1.42 on 1253 daily returns (skew 0.6, kurtosis 11.56); PSR vs zero = 0.9994. The hurdle is the Sharpe that the best of N zero-edge variants would show by luck. Every variant you or an AI agent tries raises N. That is why an agent that rewrites the rules and re-backtests in a loop will always find a 'better' strategy: it is spending this budget. Survivorship look-ahead is not in this number; the simulation page measures that separately. With the hurdle set from the parameter grid's own Sharpe spread instead, DSR = 0.998. That figure is optimistic, because neighbouring parameters are near-copies of each other.
