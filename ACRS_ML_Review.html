Critic: what the ledger says

  1. Setup B carries the system: 56 of 346 trades (16%) produce +107.5R of +230.2R. Without its 3 best trades it still makes -7.1R and it was positive in 2 of 6 years (bootstrap P(not better than the rest) = 0.138).
  2. Disaster-stop and gap exits cost -33.2R. Those entries were stretched: 13.1% above MA50 vs 3.9% for normal exits, RVOL 1.70 vs 1.35.
  3. Returns are tail-driven: the top 5% of trades supply 74% of gross profit. Any filter that skips entries has to be judged on R kept, not on hit rate.
  4. Live window: 24 closed trades, -2.61R, PF 0.59. Drawing 24 backtest trades that could have closed inside a 71-day window gives a result this bad 48% of the time (PF this low: 41%): normal for a young book. Comparing against all backtest trades, long winners included, would say 13% and wrongly suggest something broke. Only 3 Setup A entries vs 3.6 expected (P = 0.51); the live book has mostly been fed the setups that earn ~0R in the backtest too.
  5. Entry-feature drift vs backtest: none significant after Bonferroni (p < 0.0045). The machine is buying the same kind of stocks.
  6. ML entry filter (keep top 50% by model score), purged annual walk-forward: take-all +239.0R vs ridge +122.1R (IC -0.181) and logistic +101.3R (IC -0.152). Status: rejected - stays record-only.
  7. Deflated Sharpe: the 5-year curve's Sharpe 1.49 gives DSR 0.93 at the registry's 19 trials; it drops below 0.95 at about 13 independent trials. This is before the survivorship look-ahead the simulation page already measures, which lowers it further.

Step 3 · Deflated Sharpe: how many tries can this backtest afford?

Sharpe 1.49 on 1254 daily returns (skew -0.03, kurtosis 6.14); PSR vs zero = 0.9995. The hurdle is the Sharpe that the best of N zero-edge variants would show by luck. Every variant you or an AI agent tries raises N. That is why an agent that rewrites the rules and re-backtests in a loop will always find a 'better' strategy: it is spending this budget. Survivorship look-ahead is not in this number; the simulation page measures that separately. With the hurdle set from the parameter grid's own Sharpe spread instead, DSR = 0.998. That figure is optimistic, because neighbouring parameters are near-copies of each other.
