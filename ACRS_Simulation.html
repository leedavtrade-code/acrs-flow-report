⚠ Read this before believing the numbers above
Every return on this page is inflated by at least ~17 percentage points per 2-year period. Measured 2026-08-18, not estimated.

Why. The test universe is the S&P 500 as it is today. But 159 of those 503 companies (31.6%) were not in the index in 2016 — they were added later, because they did well. Backtesting 2016 with them is looking at tomorrow's newspaper. Restricting each period to the companies that were actually index members at the time costs −22.4pp per period; a random size-matched control shows only −5.1pp of that is the smaller universe, leaving −17.3pp of genuine look-ahead (t=−2.13, negative in 4 of 5 periods).

And that is the optimistic half. Companies that failed out of the index cannot be added back at all — of ten known departures (SVB, First Republic, Signature Bank, Twitter, Xilinx, Activision, Cerner, Discovery, Raytheon, CenturyLink) nine have no price history available, and the tenth returns a recycled ticker whose data begins 17 months after the bank collapsed. Adding the real failures back would push these figures down further. −17.3pp is a floor, not an estimate.

What is still trustworthy. Every comparison on this page and in the research behind it — this rule versus that rule — runs both arms on the same universe, so both carry the same bias and it cancels. The rankings hold. What does not hold is any single number read as “this is what the account would have made.”

Use this page for relative judgements, never as a return forecast.

What actually validates a low-win-rate trend system

Is the machine behaving? ?

Return is the slowest thing on this page. 5% of trades carry 74% of the gross profit, so a young book's closed trades are the losers by construction — the winners are still open — and the return stays unreadable for roughly 20 months.

Survival is a rate, not a sum, so it settles far faster. At 28 entries its confidence band is already tighter than the band on average return at 200 trades. If the live curve tracks the backtest, the machine is doing what it did in testing, whatever the P&L says this month. If it falls away, that is a real signal — and it arrives months earlier than the return does. Still-open positions are censored, not counted as failures.
