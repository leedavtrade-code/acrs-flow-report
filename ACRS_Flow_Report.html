RSRelative Strength — how a stock/sector/country is doing vs a benchmark, not its raw price change. The core ranking number everywhere on this page.
13-week RSRS measured over one calendar quarter — long enough to filter noise, short enough to catch a leader while it's still fresh (academic momentum research finds 3–12 months is the sweet spot; under ~1 month tends to reverse, over ~12 tends to be stale news).
RVOLRelative Volume — today's volume ÷ its own 20-day average. Confirms a breakout isn't happening on a quiet, meaningless day.
ATRAverage True Range — the average size of a stock's daily price swing. Sets the disaster-stop distance (2.5×ATR%) so it fits that stock's own normal noise.
MAMoving Average (e.g. "50-day MA") — the trend line. Exit = first close below it.
EODEnd of Day — every price here is from the completed daily close, never live/intraday.
SPY / ACWITicker for the S&P 500 ETF (US benchmark) / MSCI All-Country World ETF (global benchmark).
VIXThe market's "fear gauge." Shown as context only — testing found it doesn't improve entries, so it never gates a trade.

Country ranking — relative strength vs ACWI () ?

Reading the "vs SPY" columns. These are not the ETF's own return — they are how far it beat (+) or lagged (−) the S&P 500 over that window, in percentage points (pp). A figure below −100pp is normal and not an error: e.g. Indonesia over 3 years was −39% while SPY was +79%, so it lagged by −118pp. Nothing fell more than 100%.
Market phase = Weinstein Stage Analysis on the 150-day MA — a slow, ~7-month trend gauge. It deliberately ignores the 1M and 3M columns, so a market can be down sharply this quarter and still be S2 Advancing if it is well above a rising 150-day average. (Example: South Korea is −14% over 3M but +132% over 1 year, still above a 150-day MA that is rising 6%/month — that is an uptrend having a pullback, not a downtrend.) Likewise a +6% bounce inside a falling 150-day MA is still S4 Declining.

? how to read the rotation graph
Each sector plotted by strength (RS-Ratio, → right = stronger) and momentum (RS-Momentum, ↑ up = gaining). Four quadrants: Leading (strong & rising) · Weakening (strong, fading) · Lagging (weak) · Improving (weak, turning up). Sectors rotate clockwise. The arrow = direction of travel over ~3 weeks, sized by how hard it's moving. Colour matches direction: green ↗ = improving on both axes, red ↙ = fading on both, amber = mixed (e.g. a leader rolling over points amber down-right). Fixed ~7-week lookback — the timeframe buttons don't change it.

Why the ★ doesn't move when you change timeframe: the table re-sorts by whichever timeframe you pick, but the ★ (which sectors actually feed the weekly shortlist) is permanently locked to the 13-week / 3M ranking — that's the spec §5 entry gate, and it never changes with the view. So on the 1M or 1Y view you'll often see the #1-sorted sector without a ★, while a lower-sorted sector keeps it. That's correct, not a bug. The "3M rank" column shows the number the ★ is based on.

Why it's fixed at 13 weeks: one quarter is long enough to filter noise but short enough to catch leadership while it's fresh — and locking it stops the system from chasing whatever timeframe happens to look good this week.
